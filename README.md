## 🇨🇳 中文说明

**智能装备设计器 (Smart Equipment Designer)** 是一款为《钢铁雄心4》打造的深度体验优化（QoL）与自动化 Mod。它不仅能接管繁琐的装备设计流程（坦克、舰船、飞机），更能智能优化陆军编制！告别无聊的数值计算与疯狂点击，让动态评分系统为你打造最优军备。

### 🌟 核心功能
* **海陆空全覆盖**：完美支持坦克 (Tank)、飞机 (Plane) 和舰船 (Ship) 的智能设计生成。
* **智能编制设计 (Division Templates)**：突破性地加入了陆军编制智能生成与优化功能，自动为你匹配当前最佳建制。
* **动态模块评分系统 (Dynamic Scoring)**：内置深度的模块与策略评分算法 (`Module & Strategy Scorer`)。它不是套用死模板，而是根据你当前的科技水平、可用模块和战略路线，计算出当前最优解。
* **自动升级与替换**：利用内置的触发器 (On Actions)，自动为现有装备应用最新解锁的模块（火炮、引擎、雷达等），彻底解放双手。
* **原生 UI 集成**：包含精心设计的自定义 GUI 面板，无缝融入原版游戏界面。

### 🤝 兼容性
* **前置 DLC 需求**：
  * *No Step Back* (绝不后退 - 坦克设计器)
  * *By Blood Alone* (血战到底 - 飞机设计器)
  * *Man the Guns* (炮手就位 - 海军设计器)
* **Mod 兼容性**：支持大部分不大幅度修改原版科技树和模块 ID 的 Mod。
* **语言支持**：原生支持 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese) 与 英语 (English)。
* **铁人模式**：由于修改了游戏玩法逻辑与 GUI，本 Mod **不兼容**成就获取。

## 🇬🇧 English Description

**Smart Equipment Designer** is an advanced Quality of Life (QoL) and automation mod for Hearts of Iron IV. It goes beyond simple templates by utilizing a dynamic scoring system to optimize your equipment designs (Tanks, Ships, Aircraft) and even your Army Division templates! Stop manually calculating stats and let the AI build your perfect arsenal.

### 🌟 Features
* **All-in-One Designer**: Fully supports intelligent design generation for Tanks, Planes, and Ships.
* **Smart Division Templates**: Groundbreaking feature that automatically generates and optimizes your army division templates based on your current situation.
* **Dynamic Scoring & Strategy System**: Under the hood, the mod uses a custom `Module & Strategy Scorer` algorithm. It doesn't rely on static presets; instead, it dynamically evaluates your unlocked tech, available modules, and strategic path to calculate the optimal build.
* **Automated Upgrades**: Built-in triggers automatically apply newly researched modules (better guns, engines, radar, etc.) to your active designs.
* **Custom GUI**: Features carefully crafted Custom GUI elements that blend seamlessly with the vanilla interface.

### 🤝 Compatibility
* **DLC Requirements**:
  * *No Step Back* (Tank Designer)
  * *By Blood Alone* (Aircraft Designer)
  * *Man the Guns* (Naval Designer)
* **Mod Compatibility**: Compatible with most mods that do not drastically overhaul the vanilla technology tree or module IDs.
* **Languages**: Native support for English and Simplified Chinese.
* **Ironman**: Not achievement compatible, as it alters gameplay logic and custom GUIs.
